The edges go first

Nearly all of the bandwidth sits in the sharp edges, so they disperse first and ring like Fresnel fringes at a slit. Temporal dispersion and paraxial diffraction obey the same equation.

Colour is chirp

With β2 < 0 the higher frequencies travel faster, so the leading edge turns blue and the trailing edge red. Switch to normal dispersion and the sides swap.

Far field

Well past LD the power profile approaches the pulse spectrum, a sinc², because each frequency arrives at its own group delay. This is the temporal Fraunhofer limit.

Model

A(z,ω) = A(0,ω)·exp(iβ₂ω²z/2), with β₂ = −Dλ²/2πc ≈ −21.7 ps²/km. Higher-order dispersion, loss and nonlinearity are left out.
